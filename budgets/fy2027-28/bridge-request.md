## Purpose and need

Cedar Creek Bridge is a fictional crossing on a federal road used by school buses, residents, and emergency vehicles. Flood damage has restricted access. This sample request proposes a bridge-repair program that prioritizes essential routes and uses condition-based estimates.

All names, conditions, and amounts in this example are illustrative. The structured JSON contains the requested amounts; this companion document explains the business case.

## Proposed work

- Inspect damaged crossings and confirm repair scopes.
- Prioritize bridges that restore essential access.
- Design and carry out deck repairs and abutment stabilization.
- Verify completed work before approving invoices.

## Estimate basis

The request separates priority repairs, design and inspection support, and emergency-access planning. The figures are planning estimates, not contractor bids. Site inspections and procurement may change individual project costs.

## Delivery and measurement

Use quarterly delivery milestones: scope confirmation, contract award, construction, acceptance, and payment. Measure progress through accepted work and restored access, rather than money spent alone.

Cedar Creek Bridge is the worked project in the execution example: a $750,000 contract within a $1 million district allotment, followed by a $500,000 partial payment after accepted work.

## Assumptions and risks

Work depends on enacted funding, approved apportionment and allotment, completed design, and necessary approvals. Site conditions, temporary access, and seasonal work windows may affect delivery.
