# Allotment decision

> Fictional sample for the Budget Atlas prototype. Not an official document.

AL-001 · agency funds control

## Context

The agency allots $1 million of the apportioned $2.5 million to the district responsible for the bridge. The district’s allotment and its restrictions are recorded in the funds-control system.

## Sample record

- **Parent:** AP-001 · $2,500,000
- **District request:** $1,000,000

The approved $1 million allotment goes to the district responsible for Cedar Creek Bridge. The remaining $1.5 million is available for other allotments in this simplified scenario.

## Decision flow

AP-001: $2.5 million apportioned

Does the $1 million request fit the available amount, purpose, and approval conditions?

- Yes: Record AL-001: $1 million allotted
- No: Return for review; no allotment recorded

$1.5 million remains unallotted
