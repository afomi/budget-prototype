## Context

After the necessary reviews, the district signs a $750,000 bridge-repair contract. The agency records this obligation against the $1 million allotment.

## Sample record

- **Contract:** BR-12-2028 · Cedar Creek Bridge
- **Scope:** Deck repair and abutment stabilization
- **Source allotment:** AL-001 · $1,000,000
- **Recorded obligation:** $750,000
- **Unobligated allotment:** $250,000

The contract file includes approvals and the evidence supporting the obligation. The recorded amount stays within the approved allotment.
