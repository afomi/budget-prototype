01 · START WITH A SOURCE

Find the department, fund, and budget line.

In Vacaville’s FY 2025–26 budget book, Public Works’ Lighting & Landscaping Districts budget includes a functional distribution of $150,575 for Patwin Park. A related Engineer’s Report identifies Patwin Neighborhood Park as District 1, budget fund 2101.

Those are the business references a reader needs first. The row sits inside a department, a budget unit, a fiscal year, and a funding structure. A stable digital ID helps us link versions of a record; it does not replace those classifications.

The published row provides context. Our example is a fictional $10,000 maintenance work package within that baseline. We are not adding $10,000 to the published amount or assuming a transfer from another district. Actual availability and City approval requirements would need confirmation.

02 · MAKE THE REQUEST LEGIBLE

Explain what the money is meant to do.

A Public Works requester describes a small package of grounds maintenance, minor irrigation repairs, inspection, and contingency. The request says what is proposed, why it is needed, where its funding basis comes from, and what decision is being requested.

03 · REVIEW THE DECISION

Keep the question, the response, and the approved version.

A reviewer can ask a concrete question: Does this request explain its funding basis and maintenance scope clearly enough to approve? The requester revises the same documents. Earlier wording remains in the version history. Approval attaches to the current version.

In this toolkit, the exchange happens in a GitHub pull request. Comments can refer to individual lines; commits preserve revisions; a merge records approval of the prototype document. A configured independent reviewer must approve the exact current commit before the evidence check recognizes an approved document version.

    04 · FOLLOW RESPONSIBILITY

    See who carries the decision forward.

    The requester, budget reviewer, document approver, funds controller, procurement officer, contract manager, and finance staff each have a different job. The process diagram puts those actors in lanes. Its horizontal headings show the phases; the arrows show the handoffs.

    05 · CONNECT AUTHORITY TO PAYMENT

    A payment is one event in a longer history.

    The simulated work-package limit is $10,000. Funds control releases $2,500 for a period and assigns $1,000 to a work order. A procurement officer records a $750 commitment. After accepted work and an approved invoice, finance records a $500 partial payment.

    Start with PAY-001 and follow its references: payment → commitment → work order → period release → work-package limit. Each link gives a reviewer another decision and its supporting record to inspect. A later $250 payment would complete the commitment after final acceptance.

    06 · PRESERVE CHANGE; EXPOSE INCONSISTENCY

    The history should explain what changed.

    An authorized adjustment raises the period release from $2,500 to $3,000. It records AP-002 as a revision of AP-001, preserving the original decision and its receipt. Existing commitments and payments remain connected to their history.

    Now consider a new $2,000 commitment against the same work order. Only $250 remains uncommitted. The gateway should flag the inconsistency, retain the attempted source record, and leave accepted authority unchanged.

    07 · RETAIN THE RECORD AND ITS EVIDENCE

    Make the trail independently inspectable.

    The request, contract, invoice, and payment remain in their authoritative records. Verification evidence can bind selected records to their exact bytes, signer, approval, and parent references. A digest can show that retained bytes match; it cannot establish whether the original claim was true.

    A shared blockchain may help independent parties check selected approved handoffs. Draft discussion can remain with the documents. This case includes an offline UTXO plan; it does not construct or broadcast transactions. The $330M / 330,000-sat scale is experiment context. Exact cents must remain in signed data for a work package this small, with native carrier values and fees handled separately.

    The purpose of the linked document is practical: a reader can ask who decided this, which version was approved, what changed, and how does this payment relate to the funding decision? Each question should lead to an inspectable record.
